# Where your notes live

Every note is a plain-text Markdown (.md) file, so you can put them wherever you like in "Configure Workflow" (could be handy in an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) vault or a [Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com) folder, say).

- ⌃↩ in the results list reveals a note in Finder
- ⌥↩ in the results list, or ⌘⌫ in the note itself, moves it to the Trash

The results list searches every word of every note, not just the titles. Try `scratch 4471`.

Once you're done with these examples, feel free to trash them — you won't see them again 👋

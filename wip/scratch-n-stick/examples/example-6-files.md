# Where your notes live

Every note is a plain Markdown file, named for its title, so it opens in any app and is yours to keep.

- ⌃↩ in the results list shows a note in Finder.
- To keep your notes somewhere else, such as a synced folder, choose a notes folder in the workflow's configuration. They move there by themselves.
- ⌥↩ in the results list, or ⌘⌫ in the note itself, moves it to the Trash, where Put Back can rescue it.

The results list searches every word of every note, not just the titles. Try `scratch 4471`.

When you're done with the examples, trash them: they won't come back.

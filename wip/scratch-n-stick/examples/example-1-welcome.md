# Welcome to Scratch'n'Stick 🗒️

**Scratch notes** in Alfred, and **sticky notes** on your desktop.

Play with these sample notes to get a feel for it. Start by working your way through this list:

- [x] Open a note (you just did)
- [ ] Press ↩ to edit this note, then ⌘↩ to see it rendered again
- [ ] Stick it to your desktop with ⌘D
- [ ] Give it a colour with ⌘1 to ⌘6, and pick an icon from the top row
- [ ] Double-click the header bar to collapse it, and again to open it back up
- [ ] Close it with ⌘W, then bring it back with `sticky` in Alfred
- [ ] Start a note of your own with `newscratch`, or `newsticky`

Notes marked 📌💤 in the results list are sticky notes that are closed, so they'll pop straight to your desktop when you open them.

Happy scribbling.

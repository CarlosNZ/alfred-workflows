# AirDrop

## Usage

Send files, URLs or text from Alfred with AirDrop.

Select one or more files, a URL or some text in Alfred, open Universal Actions (<kbd>→</kbd> on a result, or your Universal Actions hotkey for a selection in another app) and choose **AirDrop**. Apple's AirDrop panel opens with the items already loaded: pick the device to send them to.

- **Files and folders** are sent as they are.
- **URLs** are sent as links, which open on the receiving device.
- **Text** is sent as a `.txt` file, named after its first line.

Once you've picked a device, you can press **Done** straight away: the transfer carries on in the background until it finishes.

The panel opens on whichever display macOS chooses, which may not be the one you're looking at.
